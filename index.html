About the Fest

The Qiskit Fall Fest is a collection of quantum computing events on college campuses around the world. Every Fall Fest event is organized by students, for students, and is designed to help grow the local quantum community.

Whether you are a beginner looking to run your first quantum circuit or an advanced researcher aiming to push the boundaries of current hardware, there is a track for you.

This year's event features workshops, talks, and a networking sessions, all powered by IBM Quantum and the Qiskit open-source framework.

Frequently Asked Questions

chevron_right Do I need prior quantum computing experience?

Not at all. Day 1 is designed for beginners. We'll take you from zero to running your first quantum circuit.

chevron_right Is the event free?

Yes, completely free. Registration is required so we can plan catering and materials.

chevron_right Can I attend just one day?

Absolutely. Each day is self-contained, though attending both gives you the full experience.

chevron_right What should I bring?

Nothing. We'll provide access to Lab Computers and IBM Quantum hardware, and all coding is done in the cloud.
