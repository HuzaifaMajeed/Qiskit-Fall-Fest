About the Fest

The Qiskit Fall Fest is a collection of quantum computing events on college campuses around the world. Every Fall Fest event is organized by students, for students, and is designed to help grow the local quantum community.

If you're a beginner looking to learn about quantum computing or run your first quantum algorithm, this is the event for you.

This year's student-led event features workshops, talks, and a networking sessions, all supported by IBM Quantum and the University of the Witwatersrand, Johannesburg.

Frequently Asked Questions

chevron_right Do I need prior quantum computing experience?

Not at all. This event is built for beginners with no prior knowledge. We start from scratch and work our way up together.

chevron_right Is the event free?

Completely free. We've got you covered, from refreshments to materials and IBM Quantum hardware access.

chevron_right Can I attend just one day?

Yes. Day 2 picks up where Day 1 leaves off, so attending both is essential.

chevron_right What should I bring?

Just yourself. We provide lab computers, IBM Quantum access, and everything runs in the cloud through your browser.
